# Potential Impact

• Faster Threat Detection: Identifies people, vehicles, intrusions, and security breaches in real time.

• Improved Border Response: Sends prioritized alerts with camera location, timestamp, confidence, and evidence.

• Reduced Operator Fatigue: Automates continuous CCTV monitoring and highlights relevant events.

• Stronger Evidence Protection: Encrypts snapshots and maintains tamper-evident records for investigations.

• Wider Surveillance Coverage: Enables operators to monitor multiple existing CCTV feeds from one dashboard.

• Better Accountability: Tracks alert history, user actions, model provenance, and response decisions.

# Benefits

• Social: Improves the safety of border personnel and communities by enabling earlier response to intrusion and security threats.

• Operational: Reduces manual monitoring workload, improves incident acknowledgement time, and supports faster escalation.

• Economic: Uses existing CCTV infrastructure, reducing the need for expensive smart-camera hardware and new installations.

• Security: Provides authentication, role-based access, encrypted evidence, audit logs, and cryptographic tamper detection.

• Technical: Combines computer vision, object tracking, virtual-fence rules, WebSocket alerts, telemetry, and multi-camera monitoring.

• Trust and Transparency: Uses SHA-256 hash chains, Merkle proofs, AI model provenance, and optional blockchain anchoring to verify integrity.

• Administrative: Gives supervisors searchable event history, evidence review, integrity verification, exports, and camera-health visibility.

• Scalability: Supports future expansion to additional cameras, border sectors, analytics models, C2 systems, and multi-site deployments.

• Privacy: Keeps sensitive video, snapshots, GPS data, face information, plate data, credentials, and encryption keys off-chain.

# SIH Innovation Value

• Converts standard CCTV into an intelligent border-surveillance platform.

• Combines AI detection with explainable rules and verifiable evidence.

• Adds blockchain-style trust without exposing sensitive surveillance data.

• Delivers a practical software-first solution for remote and resource-constrained border environments.

• Supports indigenous, cost-conscious, and phased national-security deployment.

# Key Outcome

IBVAP helps security teams detect threats earlier, respond faster, preserve evidence securely, and verify that critical records remain authentic.
